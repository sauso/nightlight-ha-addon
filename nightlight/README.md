# Nightlight

A private, self-hosted baby monitor for the cameras you already own: live video and audio, sleep tracking and optional alerts, without a subscription or a Nightlight-hosted video service.

> **Experimental.** Read the **Documentation** tab before you start, and please [report what you find](https://github.com/sauso/nightlight-ha-addon/issues).
>
> **Important:** Nightlight is a convenience tool, **not a safety or medical device**. Streams and alerts can fail. It must never replace adult supervision or be relied on to detect a child in distress.

This app runs Nightlight 0.49.0 with host networking. It opens in its own browser tab on port 4000, not in the Home Assistant sidebar. More about Nightlight: [github.com/sauso/nightlight](https://github.com/sauso/nightlight).
